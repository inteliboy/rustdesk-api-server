Strategies

A strategy is a set of RustDesk client settings pushed to devices: it is assigned to a device (on its page) or to a group, and reaches the client with its next heartbeat. Settings a strategy leaves unset are reset to the client’s own default. The default strategy is what a device gets when it has none of its own and none through its group — a baseline for every new device.
